FAQs

Are you accepting new clients?

The published FAQ indicates that small and medium breeds may be accepted. Larger breeds are considered depending on availability.

How often should I book a groom?

Longer coats generally benefit from grooming every four to six weeks. Coat care between appointments can help prevent knots.

How long does grooming take?

Allow approximately two to four hours. The team will advise on collection timing based on your dog’s needs.

What happens if my dog is matted?

The groomer will assess your dog’s coat and recommend a comfortable approach. Additional work may affect the estimate.

Do I need vaccination records?

Dogs need to be fully vaccinated. New clients should bring or provide vaccination records for their first visit.

When can my puppy have its first groom?

Once fully vaccinated, usually around 16 weeks, a gentle introductory wash and tidy may be suitable.

Can I book an appointment online?

This proposal demonstrates a structured appointment request. The salon would still need to review requests and confirm appointments. No real booking is available in this demo.

How should I manage future appointments?

The salon encourages customers to arrange future visits when collecting their dogs. Availability can be limited.
