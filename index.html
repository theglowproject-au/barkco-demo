Helpful information

Before your visit.

Can I book instantly?

In this proposed workflow, requests are reviewed by the salon. No appointments are confirmed by this demo.

Do you accept new clients?

The salon's published FAQ says it accepts new small and medium dogs; larger dogs are assessed depending on capacity.

How long does a groom take?

The salon estimates two to four hours, depending on the individual dog's coat and temperament.

What if my dog's coat is matted?

The groomer needs to assess the coat and discuss the most comfortable option. Extra work may incur additional fees.

Do vaccinations need to be up to date?

The salon requires fully vaccinated dogs and asks for proof on the first visit.
